Courses we offer

Northwise tutors cover the standard middle and high school math sequence. Each tutor has already completed the course they teach and works from a structured curriculum for it.

Not sure which course to pick?

Choose the course the student is taking at school. If the school uses different names, such as Math 7 or Integrated Math, pick the closest match or choose "Other" on the signup form and describe it. Then list the specific topics the student wants help with.
