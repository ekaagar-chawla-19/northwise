Safety

Tutor code of conduct

Every Northwise tutor reads and agrees to this code before working with students. Most of our students are minors, so these rules are strict on purpose. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask us first.

Sessions

  • Hold sessions only online, using the meeting link set up for that session and shared with the parent or guardian.
  • Every session is group-based or held where a parent or guardian can see and hear it. Never run a one-on-one session out of a parent's view.
  • Schedule sessions only through the parent or guardian. Do not change a session time directly with a student.
  • Start and end on time. Sessions last about 1.5 hours.
  • Keep your camera on, sit somewhere quiet, and make sure your background and clothing are appropriate.
  • Do not record a session, or take screenshots of students, without written permission from the parent or guardian and from Northwise.

Communication

  • Do not message students privately. That includes texts, direct messages, email, gaming chats, and any other app.
  • Send all scheduling and updates to the parent or guardian email, with Northwise copied.
  • Do not share your personal phone number or social media accounts with students, and do not follow, friend, or accept requests from them.
  • If a student contacts you directly, do not reply privately. Tell Northwise, and we will follow up with the parent or guardian.

Respect

  • Be patient and encouraging. Never make a student feel stupid for asking a question.
  • Use respectful, school-appropriate language. No swearing, insults, or inappropriate jokes.
  • Treat every student fairly, whatever their background, ability, or identity. Harassment and discrimination are never allowed.
  • Keep conversations focused on math and studying. Do not ask students about personal matters that are not related to their learning.

Academic honesty

  • Help students understand the material. Do not do graded homework, projects, or tests for them.
  • Do not share copies of real tests, quizzes, or answer keys from any teacher or school. You can explain what topics tend to come up and how to prepare, but not the actual questions.
  • Do not help a student with a test or quiz while they are taking it.

Privacy

  • Keep student and family information private. Use it only to run sessions.
  • Do not post about students or sessions online, and do not share their names, schools, or work with anyone outside Northwise.

Boundaries

  • Do not meet students in person outside of Northwise.
  • Do not accept money or gifts from families, and do not offer paid tutoring to Northwise students.
  • Do not give students rides, food, or anything else outside of tutoring.

Reporting concerns

  • If a student tells you they are being hurt, or you see something that worries you about their safety, tell Northwise right away. Do not promise to keep it secret.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 first.
  • If you see another tutor break this code, tell Northwise at ekaagar.chawla@gmail.com.

If the code is broken

  • Northwise may pause or end a tutor's involvement for breaking this code.
  • Serious concerns about a student's safety may be shared with the student's parent or guardian, and with the appropriate authorities when needed.

Parents and guardians can read this code too. If you have a question or concern about a session, email ekaagar.chawla@gmail.com.
