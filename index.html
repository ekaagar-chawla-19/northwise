How it works

Sign up, hear from us, attend sessions.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    A parent or guardian fills out a short form with the student's math course, the topics they need help with, and when they are free.

  2. 2

    Hear from us

    We email the parent or guardian to talk through what the student needs and set a session time that works for your family and the tutor.

  3. 3

    Attend sessions

    Sessions are online and run about 1.5 hours. Every session is group-based or visible to a parent, and tutors follow a shared curriculum.

Safety

How we keep sessions safe.

These rules apply to every session and every tutor.

Group-based or parent-visible

Sessions either take place in a group or where a parent or guardian can see and hear them. Tutors do not meet students one-on-one out of view.

No private messaging

Tutors and students do not message each other directly (unless consented by the parent). Scheduling and updates go to the parent or guardian email you give us.

Code of conduct

Every tutor agrees to the Northwise tutor code of conduct and is trained on it before working with students.

Raising a concern

If anything about a session worries you, email ekaagar.chawla@gmail.com. A parent or guardian can stop tutoring at any time.
