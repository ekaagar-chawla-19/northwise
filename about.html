How it started

Started by a high school student in Cupertino.

Northwise was founded by Ekaagar Chawla, a student at Monta Vista High School in Cupertino, California.

Northwise is a free, student-run community tutoring initiative. There is no cost to families. The tutors are volunteers: high school and college students who have recently taken the courses they teach.

Northwise was created to give students rigorous, high-quality math help without the cost. We believe every student has a right to a good education, and no family should have to pay a high price for that right.

We focus on math for grades 7 to 11. Tutors teach from a structured curriculum and are trained before they start working with students, so a student gets consistent help no matter which tutor they work with.
