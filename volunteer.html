Volunteer as a math tutor

Northwise tutors are high school and college students who have recently taken the courses they teach. You get a structured curriculum and training before you start working with students. All fields are required unless marked optional.

What you get

  • A structured curriculum for each course you teach, so you are not planning sessions from scratch.
  • Training before your first session on how to run a session and on our safety rules.

What we ask

  • Show up for the sessions you commit to. Sessions are online and last about 1.5 hours.
  • Keep every session group-based or parent-visible.
  • Do not message students privately. Scheduling goes through parents or guardians.
  • Follow the tutor code of conduct.
Northwise

Northwise is a free, student-run community tutoring initiative. We offer free math help, from school-level courses up to advanced topics, for students in grades 7 to 11 who would otherwise go without.
