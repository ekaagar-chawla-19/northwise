Questions and answers

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Northwise tutoring is free for every student. There are no fees of any kind.

Who are the tutors?

Trained high school and college volunteers who have recently taken the courses they teach. Before working with students, every tutor completes training and agrees to our code of conduct. Tutors teach from a structured curriculum, so sessions follow a clear plan.

Who can sign up?

Any student in grades 7 to 11. A parent or guardian needs to fill out or review the signup form and give permission.

What do sessions look like?

Sessions are online and last about 1.5 hours. We schedule them with the parent or guardian, based on the tutor's availability and the times that work best for your family.

Each session is either group-based or held where a parent or guardian can see and hear it. The tutor works through the topics the student needs, using the Northwise curriculum for that course.

How do you keep students safe?

Sessions are group-based or parent-visible. Tutors and students do not message each other privately; scheduling goes through the parent or guardian. Every tutor follows our code of conduct. Read our safety rules.

What if we need to cancel a session?

Please give your tutor notice as early as you can, through the parent or guardian email. Regular attendance matters, because tutors set aside time for each student.

How can I support Northwise?

The most useful things are to volunteer as a tutor if you have recently taken these courses, and to tell families who could use free math help that we exist. If you can offer a space for group sessions, please email us.
