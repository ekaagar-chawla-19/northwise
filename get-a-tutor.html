What we ask of families

  • Attend scheduled sessions and arrive on time.
  • Give notice as early as you can if you need to cancel.
  • Tell us if the student's schedule or course changes.

Your answers are sent directly to Northwise. Questions? Email ekaagar.chawla@gmail.com.

What happens next

We read your form and email the parent or guardian to set up a session time, based on a tutor's availability and the times you prefer.

Sessions are online and last about 1.5 hours.

Safety

Sessions are group-based or parent-visible, tutors do not message students privately, and every tutor follows a code of conduct. Read our safety rules.
